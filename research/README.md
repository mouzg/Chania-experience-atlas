# Crete Experience Research · Start Small / Profit First (v7)

The complete 111-record research database (public operator links, prices, locations, mountain classifications, popularity and business age) remains intact. This version adds a first-stage launch planner, competitor-derived feasibility heuristics, concepts, a break-even calculator, and launch prioritization in the main Explore view. Example prices and gear envelopes for our business are hypothetical and are not verified operator costs.

Only public venue coordinates are included. The private comparison base and financial inputs are stored locally in each browser, not in GitHub.

Upload the contents of this zip at the root of `mouzg/Chania-experience-atlas`; it updates `research/index.html` and `research/data/experiences.json`. No homepage files are replaced. GitHub Pages deployment will happen on the normal repo branch.

Regulatory preconditions: confirm vehicle-with-driver legal basis, food/hygiene, insurance, access, emergency preparedness and water potability before accepting paid bookings. References: https://mitos.gov.gr/ and https://eugo.gov.gr/services/375869.
