Database & publishing

The downloadable JSON and CSV are the portable research source of truth. Add or merge real experiences here without rewriting the entire website.

Privacy: This is a research database built from public operator listings. Do not enter personal property coordinates, routes, village identity or confidential business plans in records you intend to publish. Notes are local to this browser unless you deliberately export them.

How updates work

1. Weekly research provides new or changed listings.

2. Use Import / merge JSON to update this browser. Same ID or source/title records are merged rather than duplicated.

3. Export the full JSON, replace data/experiences.json in your GitHub repository, and push. Your public website updates from that file automatically.

Methodology

Published prices are starting advertised prices in EUR and can change. Per-person, per-vehicle and private group prices are explicitly distinguished. Empty price means unverified.

Original photographic thumbnails are hotlinked only for sources that were located; other cards link directly to the operator's own photo page. No stock images or AI-generated photos are represented as competitor photos.

Research checked: 2026-11-11. Some tours are seasonal, sold out, or require advance confirmation.
